The Chef

Béchamel for breakfast at six years old.

I learned to cook on a stool beside my grandmother in Bozeman, Montana, and went on to cook wild game, foraged mushrooms and native grains everywhere from a permaculture farm in India to the coast of Australia. These days I live in Avon with my Italian wife, who loves food as much as I do.
