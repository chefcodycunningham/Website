Bio

Cody Cunningham

I learned to cook on a stool beside my grandmother, and was making béchamel for breakfast at six. Growing up in Bozeman, Montana meant wild game, foraged mushrooms and mountain berries, and I've cooked that way ever since.

The work has taken me a long way from Montana: a nonprofit café on a permaculture farm in Tamil Nadu, cooking with millets and native grains; kitchens in Melbourne and Byron Bay, where I fell for lemon myrtle and bush pepper; then back to the mountains, at Sweet Basil and Mountain Standard, and as executive chef of Larkspur in Vail.

Now I cook in people's homes. My wife is Italian, we live in Avon, and we go back to Italy every year to eat. Her mother's tiramisù is on my menus.
