About Cody

Learned on a stool in my grandmother's kitchen

I was making béchamel for breakfast at six. Cooking has been my life since, and it has taken me around the world.

I grew up in Bozeman, Montana, foraging mushrooms and berries and cooking wild game. In India I started a nonprofit café on a permaculture farm, cooking with native grains and millets few chefs ever see. In Australia I ran kitchens in Byron Bay and Melbourne, working with lemon myrtle, bush pepper and river mint. Then came Vail's best kitchens, and Larkspur.

Now my Italian wife and I live in Avon. We go back to Italy every year and come home at least 15 pounds heavier.

Questions

Before you book

Where do you cook?

Vail, Beaver Creek, Avon, Edwards, Eagle, Summit County and Aspen. I cook in private homes, vacation rentals and condos. If there's a stove, we can make it work.

Are groceries included?

Yes. The per-guest price covers ingredients, cooking, service and cleanup. Premium add-ons like caviar or A5 wagyu are quoted separately. confirm

Can you handle allergies and dietary needs?

Always. Gluten-free, dairy-free, vegetarian, vegan, and serious allergies. Tell me in your inquiry and I'll build the menu around them.

How far ahead should I book?

Two to four weeks is usually fine. For Christmas through New Year's, Presidents' Day and spring break, book as early as you can.

What's the deposit and cancellation policy?

A 50% deposit holds your date, refundable up to 14 days before the dinner. placeholder

Do you provide wine?

You're welcome to provide your own, and I'm happy to suggest pairings. Or add a sommelier-guided pairing to your dinner.
